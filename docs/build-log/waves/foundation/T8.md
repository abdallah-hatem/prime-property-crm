## Endpoints

`GET/POST /listings` · `POST /listings/assign` · `GET/PATCH/DELETE /listings/:id` ·
`POST /listings/:id/status` · `PUT /listings/:id/ad` · `POST /listings/:id/ad/check-in` ·
`GET/POST /listings/:id/notes` · `POST /listings/:id/photos` · `PUT /listings/:id/photos/order` ·
`DELETE /listings/:id/photos/:photoId` · `POST /uploads/listing-photo`.

- Search: every contract filter. `status` defaults to AVAILABLE. A min above its max is a 400.
  `checkInDue` means an open check-in due before the end of today (Cairo), the same as
  `/me/today`. `q` matches area, compound, address, description and owner name (`escapeLike`), or
  phone digits. Phone digits match only the caller's own listings unless they hold
  `listings.manageAll`. Rows never carry the phone or the owner name.
- Writes (edit, status, ad, check-in, notes, photos, upload token) need the listing assigned to
  the caller or `listings.manageAll`. Anyone else gets a 403. A missing or trashed id is a 404.
  Each write locks the listing row (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`), so two status changes at once open one
  take-down task and two photo registrations at once can't pass 30.
- Create: from an owner (`ownerId`, own owner, or any owner with `owners.viewAll`) or direct
  (`listings.create`), never both. Direct: the phone is checked against live listings
  (`PHONE_IN_LISTING`), then trashed owners (`PHONE_IN_TRASH` with `trash.restore`, else
  `PHONE_EXISTS`). A live owner holding it is linked: it becomes LISTED and moves to the listing's
  member through `reassignOwnerAndListing`, with one ASSIGN entry. The owner row is locked, and a
  lost race on the live-listing unique indexes maps to the same 409.
- Ad and status rules live in `listings.pure.ts` (`adChange`, `statusEffects`, `checkInRefusal`).
  Check-ins use `RemindersService.addListingTask` and `closeOpenReminders(…, kinds)`.
- Delete (`listings.manageAll`): closes every open reminder, `TrashService.softDelete` (logs
  DELETE), and the owner goes LISTED → INTERESTED.
- Photos: the URL must be https on our Blob host, with no credentials or port. Its decoded path
  must equal `pathname`, one file directly under `listings/<id>/` (`PHOTO_URL_INVALID`).
  Registering the same URL twice returns the first photo. A reorder must list every live photo
  exactly once (`PHOTO_SET_MISMATCH`).
- Upload handshake: accepts only the `blob.generate-client-token` event. `clientPayload` must be
  `{"listingId"}` of a listing the caller may change, and the pathname must sit in that listing's
  folder (`UPLOAD_REFUSED`). It signs a token for jpeg/png/webp, 10 MB, random suffix, through
  `@vercel/blob/client` `handleUpload`. The answer is raw, not enveloped. No `onUploadCompleted`:
  the web registers the photo with `POST /listings/:id/photos`. Without a token or host:
  `503 UPLOADS_NOT_CONFIGURED`.

## Decisions and deviations

- **Not-yours on a listing is 403, not 404**: listings are visible to everyone, so a 404 hides
  nothing. The plan's cases say 403. A missing or trashed listing is still 404.
- **Default member**: from an owner, the owner's member (the creator for a member's own owner).
  Direct, the creator, and a linked lead moves to them. The spec says only "default: the creator".
- **PATCH `ownerPhone`** is allowed (the contract lists every create field). It refuses a phone of
  another live listing (`PHONE_IN_LISTING`) or of another owner (`PHONE_EXISTS`). It never re-links.
- **Ad**: an omitted `link` keeps the stored one, and `null` clears it. Saving Live again adds no
  check-in. NOT_POSTED closes the check-in and clears the dates, but leaves a take-down task open.
  TAKEN_DOWN → LIVE again restarts check-ins. `ListingAd` gains `takenDownAt` (additive).
- **Check-in on a Live ad of a non-Available listing** → `400 LISTING_NOT_AVAILABLE` (the business
  doc: no check-ins after it stops being Available).
- **Back to Available with a Live ad** schedules a check-in. The take-down task stays open (it
  closes only on Taken down).
- **Restore of a listing** also refuses `PHONE_IN_LISTING` / `PHONE_EXISTS`. It re-creates the Live
  ad's check-in (or a take-down task) and joins the owner's member.
- **Upload handshake doesn't check the 30-photo limit** (registration does). That avoids a module
  cycle and keeps the handshake read-only. `upload-completed` callbacks are refused (400).
- **New env var** `BLOB_STORE_HOST` (optional; default: both public and private hosts derived from
  `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN`'s store id). Both are in `.env.example`. No schema change.
- New dependency `@vercel/blob` (2.8.2) in `apps/api`; `require('@vercel/blob/client')` loads as
  CommonJS (no ESM trap on Vercel).
- `docs/BUSINESS_LOGIC.md`: new paragraph "Listings, ads and photos (built, T8)".

## Shared pieces

- `components/ui/scroll-cue.tsx` (new): `useScrollEdges(axis)` tracks which ends of a scroll box
  still hide content (scroll listener + `ResizeObserver`; RTL's negative `scrollLeft` read by
  magnitude; 1 px slack so zoom rounding never draws a cue), and `ScrollEdgeCues` draws the fades
  with logical `start`/`end`, so they mirror under Arabic. Nothing overflows → no cue.
- `components/ui/select.tsx`: the list hangs below its trigger (`position="popper"`,
  `side="bottom"`, `align="start"`, `sideOffset` 6, `collisionPadding` 8), trigger-wide, capped at
  `min(available height, 60dvh)` and scrolling its own viewport. Radix's collision flip turned a
  list upward whenever the whole list didn't fit below — on a phone sheet that covers the form —
  so the side is chosen once on open from the trigger's position: below when at least 120 px
  (two and a half rows) are free there or below is the roomier side, else above. Every Select in
  the app gets it, inside dialogs and sheets too.

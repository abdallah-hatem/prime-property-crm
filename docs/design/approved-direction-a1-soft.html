Switchboard Soft

The Switchboard layout you picked, restyled. The counter board, the urgency marker on each row and the big Call action all stay in place. The boxes are gone: rounded cards with soft layered shadows, pill chips and buttons, a refined indigo-cobalt, and amber kept only for urgency. Motion is a smooth 200–250ms ease-out.

Palette

Indigo-cobalt, with a slight shift toward violet across a gradient, is the only colour that means "act": Call, save, the active tab. Amber appears only when something is waiting on you. The overdue counter and urgency pills carry the red; nothing else does.

Type scale

Plus Jakarta Sans is geometric and friendly, with tight, modern numerals for the counters and prices. IBM Plex Sans Arabic has the same open, even texture, so mixed lines read as one voice.

Screens

Phone width, 390px; each screen scrolls inside its frame. The urgency marker is now a rounded pill at the row's start edge: red for overdue, indigo for due today, amber for a listing check-in.

Motion

Soft press

Buttons scale to 96–97% and their shadow settles in 200ms, ease-out. Nothing bounces.

Counters rise

Board numbers fade up 6px, 50ms apart, when Today opens or a count changes. The rows don't animate.

Live ripples

A Live ad's dot sends out a soft ring every two seconds. Sheets slide up in 250ms and close in 150ms.
